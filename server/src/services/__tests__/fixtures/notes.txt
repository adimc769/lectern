CHAPTER 1: CELL STRUCTURE

The cell is the basic unit of life. Every living organism is composed of one or more cells that carry out the essential functions of growth and reproduction.

Organelles include the nucleus, mitochondria, and ribosomes. Each organelle performs a specialized role that keeps the cell alive and functioning properly.

SECTION 2: ENERGY FLOW

Photosynthesis converts light energy into chemical energy stored in glucose molecules. Cellular respiration then releases that energy for cellular work and maintenance.
